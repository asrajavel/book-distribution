Privacy

Book Distribution is used by ISKCON temple volunteers to record the books they distribute.

What we keep: your Google account email, the name and phone number you enter, and the distribution events and books you record. Places you save have a name and a map position.

How it is used: only to run the app and for the temple's own records. Your phone number and email are visible only to the app's admins. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else, and there are no ads or trackers.

Location: read only when you start an event or add a new place, to suggest the nearest place. It is not stored or tracked.

Removal: ask a temple admin to delete your details.

Data is stored with Supabase. Sign-in is with Google.
